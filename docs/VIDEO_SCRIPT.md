### [00:00 - 00:45] Act 1: The Core Engineering Problem & Architectural Philosophy
**Visual**: Title slide with architecture diagram (`run_pipeline.py`, streaming ijson with `yajl2_c`, zero data invention, memory bounded < 2.0 GB).

"Judges and colleagues: The Transparency in Coverage mandate created massive, unwieldy data lakes—hundreds of gigabytes of raw, redundant JSON. Most pipelines fail because they try to `json.load()` entire files into memory, inflate address verification numbers using circular self-matching, or invent synthetic data when facing coverage gaps.

Our mission was to build a hackathon-winning, production-grade data curation pipeline for Centene’s California Commercial Group provider rates under strict ground rules: zero invented data, zero hardcoded paths outside `config.yaml`, and complete, verifiable lineage for every single row.

Every single figure I present today is generated by code directly from the output artifacts in our repository."

### [00:45 - 01:30] Act 2: Phase 1 & 2: Plan Scoping & Explainable PPO Classification
**Visual**: Terminal output / Slide showing Index parsing and PPO classification breakdown.

"We began by parsing 4 California monthly index files, uncovering 3,275 raw plan entries deduplicated into 3,271 unique plan-file associations in 0.04 seconds.

Our explainable PPO classifier evaluated plan names using word-boundary regexes, exclusion heuristics, and multi-token conflict resolvers. We classified 1,312 California group plans:
- **453 PPO plans identified** pointing to a 701 MB monthly machine-readable rate file.
- **719 HMO plans cleanly excluded**.
- **127 Exclusive Open Access (EOA) plans** isolated.

Crucially, our sensitivity analysis revealed that including or excluding EOA plans yields a delta of exactly zero rates: Centene bundles all California commercial products into one shared schedule. We therefore truthfully label the deliverable `CA_COMMERCIAL_GROUP_SHARED_SCHEDULE` and track contract confidence under two explicit tiers: T1 for explicit PPO (182 keys) and T2 for shared commercial (27,377 keys)."

### [01:30 - 02:30] Act 3: Phase 3 & 4: Streaming Rate Extraction, Real Quality Grading, & Outliers
**Visual**: Terminal streaming output, raw duplicate breakdown, 8-metric quality scorecard.

"We streamed Centene’s 701 MB rate file using `ijson` with the C-based `yajl2_c` backend and DuckDB reference indexing, keeping memory strictly under 1.4 GB. 

Our profiling revealed that 97.90% of the 1.51 million raw prices are structural fan-out duplicates across provider reference groups, while true within-block duplicates are only 0.76% (11,432 rows).

Our hierarchical tie-breaking engine resolved the file down to exactly **27,559 curated rate keys**, disambiguating complex multi-rate lines such as Revenue Code 636 drug infusions via `additional_information`.

We then graded the raw stream across the 8 TiC quality metrics:
- Overall File Quality Score: **91.14 / 100.00** (Letter Grade A).
- HiLabs Benchmark Procedure Coverage: **68.81% weighted coverage** across 134 matching high-volume procedure codes (**22.98% unweighted distinct code coverage**).
- Combined Final Score: **82.21 / 100.00 (Letter Grade B)**.

We also executed a peer median sanity audit: flagging 1,064 statistical outliers, quoting their exact raw JSON objects without speculation, capping them at `MEDIUM` reliability, and exporting `rate_outlier_review.csv`."

### [02:30 - 03:30] Act 4: Phase 5 & 6: Non-Circular Address Linkage & Production Deliverables
**Visual**: Address Linkage Verification table (5 mutually exclusive tiers summing to 100%), hold-out precision chart, and Excel multi-sheet preview.

"Address linkage is where ordinary pipelines produce misleading numbers. Because 97% of provider groups in Centene’s network have only 1 NPI, treating TIN co-membership as independent corroboration is completely circular.

We engineered a strictly non-circular entity resolution engine with 5 mutually exclusive tiers summing to exactly 100.00%:
- **Verified-Strict**: 289 NPIs (7.82%) corroborated across $\ge 2$ independent source groups.
- **Verified-TIN-corroborated**: 204 NPIs (5.52%) backed by multi-NPI organizations.
- **Probable**: 1,234 NPIs (33.41%) backed by fresh single-source records updated within 3 years.
- **Unverified**: 1,966 NPIs (53.22%) comprising stale single-source records (>3 years old) or deactivated NPIs.
- **Facility/TIN-level**: 1 NPI / 5 rows (0.03%) for facility-only fee rows, capped strictly at facility level.

We measured hold-out precision across N=302 overlapping clinicians against CMS Doctors & Clinicians:
- Strict Verified: **100.00% precision** (95% CI: [98.1%, 100.0%])
- TIN-corroborated: **83.33% precision** (95% CI: [55.2%, 95.3%])
- Probable: **76.00% precision** (95% CI: [65.2%, 84.2%])
- Unverified: **0.00% precision** (95% CI: [0.0%, 21.5%])

Our production deliverable provides **69,144 multi-site rows** across both the clean **6-column problem-statement view** (`final_rates_ps_grain.csv` and Sheet 1) and the full **42-column lossless relational view** (Parquet, CSV, and Sheet 2)."

### [03:30 - 04:30] Act 5: Challenge C7: Cost-Effectiveness Provider Frontier & Regional Benchmarking
**Visual**: Interactive Streamlit app showing county cost-efficiency curves and provider frontier rankings.

"For Challenge C7, we mapped California providers across 19 geographical county clusters and established specialty peer groups.

We computed median benchmark rates for each procedure and derived Provider Efficiency Indices:
- Providers with index < 1.0 deliver high-value, cost-effective care below regional peer medians.
- Providers with index > 1.0 represent high-cost contracting outliers.
- We integrated automated regional-to-statewide fallbacks to protect against thin provider samples.

Our interactive Streamlit dashboard allows actuaries and network managers to visually explore cost-efficiency frontiers, compare contracted rates against peer distributions, and filter by verified practicing addresses in real time."
